About Me

Making you look
& feel your best

Hi, I'm Sarah, a mobile hair and makeup artist based in Farnborough, covering Hampshire, Surrey, and beyond. Whatever the occasion, my aim is simple: to ensure you look and feel absolutely beautiful.

I specialise in bridal and bridal-party makeup, day and evening looks, group makeup parties, and one-to-one makeup lessons. As a mobile artist I come to you, so you can relax in the comfort of your own home on your most special days.
